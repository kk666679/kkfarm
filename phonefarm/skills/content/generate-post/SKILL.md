# Generate Post (2026 Edition)

## Core Philosophy

**Authenticity over polish.** In 2026, AI-generated content has saturated feeds, making "human touch" the scarcest and most valuable commodity. Posts that feel raw, imperfect, and genuinely human outperform overly produced content .

**Platform-native, not cross-posted.** Each platform behaves like a distinct media channel. Copy-paste execution fails; the same message must be reshaped to fit platform-specific behaviors and audience expectations .

## Procedure

### 1. Analyze Platform & Topic
- Identify target platform and its current algorithm priorities
- Determine audience segment (generational preferences vary significantly) 
- Assess topic suitability for platform (growth vs. engagement vs. conversion)

### 2. Generate Text Content with Platform-Specific Formatting
- **Front-load the hook** within visible character thresholds
- Apply optimal post length (shorter outperforms longer) 
- Include platform-native features (polls, carousels, DM triggers)

### 3. Generate Hashtags (Strategic, Not Stuffed)
- Use 3-5 hashtags maximum across platforms (Instagram now rewards fewer, more relevant tags)
- Mix broad discovery tags with niche community tags
- Avoid generic/banned tags that signal spam 

### 4. Create Image/Video Prompts if Needed
- Apply 2026 visual trends (see Anti-patterns for current aesthetics)
- For image generation: leverage photo-based personalization trends
- Prioritize "raw/handheld" aesthetics over studio-polished looks 

### 5. Apply Brand Voice with Human Touch
- Incorporate "controlled imperfection" — slightly unpolished phrasing, natural pauses
- Reference real-world context (timely moments, behind-the-scenes)
- Avoid the "AI slop" tone: generic, overly enthusiastic, syntactically perfect 

### 6. Validate Against Platform Policies & 2026 Trends
- Check character limits (see Reference section)
- Verify hashtag counts within platform-specific limits
- Confirm content aligns with authenticity mandate (no pure AI-generated posts without human voice)

## Anti-Patterns (2026 Updated)

### ❌ Never Do
- **Pure AI-generated content without human touch** — audiences and algorithms both penalize this 
- **Exact content reuse across accounts** — platform spam detection is more aggressive
- **Exceeding platform character limits** — causes truncation or posting failure
- **Over-polished production** — audiences distrust "too perfect" brand content 
- **Hashtag stuffing** — Instagram and TikTok now penalize excessive tags; optimal is 3-5 
- **Ignoring platform-native formats** — Reels on Instagram, carousels for engagement, live video for Facebook reach 
- **Generic "AI slop" aesthetics** — syntactically perfect, emotionally empty content 

### ✅ Do Instead
- **Lead with human authenticity** — raw, handheld, unscripted feels 
- **Use platform-native features** — Threads topic tags (not hashtags), DM automation for conversion 
- **Create repeatable content series** — audiences follow recognizable formats 
- **Leverage on-screen text as design** — 85% watch without sound 
- **Test with Trial Reels** — validate hooks with non-followers first 

## Platform-Specific Guidelines (2026)

| Facebook | 40-80 chars for engagement | 1-2 | Live video gets 4× links, 6× images  |
| Threads | Under 200 chars | 1 topic tag | Conversational, not hashtag-driven  |

## 2026 Visual Trends for Image Prompts

When generating image/video prompts, incorporate current trends:

- **"Ugly/NS Paint" aesthetic** — intentionally crude, childlike, poorly drawn style that's "so bad it's cute" 
- **Photo-based personalization** — dual-exposure travel posters, mini-me worlds, anime conversions 
- **Raw/handheld video** — Snapchat-style unfiltered capture 
- **Text-as-design** — captions and on-screen text as graphic elements
