# Generate Video Script (2026 Edition)

## Core Philosophy

**The hook is the video.** In 2026, short-form platforms have compressed the attention window to the first **1-3 seconds**, and the algorithm tests your content against cold audiences immediately — not just your existing followers . A video with a weak hook will stall regardless of production quality .

**Short wins, despite longer caps.** Platforms now allow videos up to 3-20 minutes, but engagement data consistently rewards **under 60 seconds**, frequently **under 30** . Length caps are a ceiling, not a target.

## Procedure

### 1. Identify Hook (First 1-3 Seconds)
This is non-negotiable. The hook determines distribution more than any other element .

**2026 Hook Patterns That Work:**
- **How-to with specific outcome**: "How to film a week of content in 90 minutes" beats generic promises 
- **Social proof with result**: "I went from 200 to 40,000 followers in six months" — specific, credible numbers only 
- **Curiosity gap**: Tease an answer the viewer must keep watching to get 
- **Pattern interrupt**: Unexpected visual or angle that breaks feed rhythm 
- **Answer-first**: Meet search intent immediately, then expand — TikTok is now a discovery engine, not passive feed 

**Critical 2026 nuance**: On Instagram Reels, the first frame must work **on mute** — significant portion watch with sound off, so on-screen text in the first second is standard . On TikTok, raw conversational delivery with a spoken hook outperforms overproduction .

### 2. Structure: Hook → Value → CTA
- **Hook** (0-3s): Earn the next three seconds 
- **Value** (3-25s): Deliver the promised outcome. No dead air. Quick-cut pacing 
- **CTA** (final 2-5s): Platform-native, not generic. See CTA section below.

**2026 editing standard**: 8-15 rapid cuts in a 30-second window, captions synced to music beats or voiceover pauses .

### 3. Write Scene-by-Scene Breakdown
For each scene, specify:
- **Visual**: What's on screen (shot type, subject, movement)
- **Audio**: Spoken line, sound effect, or music cue
- **Text overlay**: On-screen text that reinforces or adds context
- **Timing**: Duration in seconds

**Authenticity mandate**: Raw, handheld, Snapchat-style footage consistently outperforms studio-polished content. Real process videos, behind-the-scenes, and "work-in-public" formats are dominant .

### 4. Add Timing Annotations
Total target: **15-45 seconds** for maximum completion rate. Under 30 seconds is optimal for viral potential .

- Hook: 1-3s
- Setup/context: 3-8s
- Core value: 8-25s
- CTA: 2-5s

### 5. Include Captions/Overlay Text
**85% of viewers watch without sound** — captions are mandatory, not optional .

2026 trend: **Text as design element**. Text interventions that follow speech as a graphic element, perfectly timed to beats, are being used creatively rather than just as subtitles .

### 6. Suggest Music/Sound Trends
- **Trending audio extends reach** but is not a substitute for a hook 
- **Easy-to-copy formats travel fastest** — simple movement hooks, reaction formats, quick edit templates 
- **Comment-driven remixes**: Formats that start in the comments (photo reacts, stitches, duets) can outperform forced trend copies 

## Anti-Patterns (2026 Updated)

### ❌ Never Do
- **Skip the hook** — 3 seconds is the hard ceiling for attention
- **Make videos >60s for short-form** — completion rate collapses beyond this 
- **Use generic CTAs** ("like and follow") — they signal low effort
- **Over-produce** — audiences distrust overly polished brand content; raw outperforms 
- **Ignore the mute viewer** — 85% watch without sound 
- **Force a trend** — comment-driven participation formats outperform forced copies 
- **Pure AI-generated slop** — AI is a production accelerator, not a creative replacement 

### ✅ Do Instead
- **Lead with specific outcome**: "How to X in Y minutes" 
- **Show the process, not just the result**: Real-process, work-in-public, accountability content is dominant 
- **Use Trial Reels on Instagram**: Test hooks with non-followers before wide distribution 
- **Build repeatable series**: Familiar formats perform better and solve creative fatigue 
- **Make it loopable**: Wellness content sees 116% average view percentage because viewers replay guided routines 

## Platform-Specific Script Guidelines (2026)

| Platform | Optimal Duration | Hook Style | Key 2026 Behavior |
|----------|-----------------|------------|-------------------|
| TikTok | 15-30s | Raw, conversational, spoken | Discovery-first; 58% discover brands here  |
| Instagram Reels | 15-45s | Visual pattern interrupt + on-screen text | 75% of recommendations are original posts  |
| YouTube Shorts | 20-60s | How-to, specific outcome | Search-adjacent; completion rate matters most  |

## CTA Framework (2026)

**Platform-native CTAs only:**
- TikTok: "Comment [word] and I'll send you the full guide" (DM trigger)
- Instagram: "Save this for later" or "Share to your story if you agree"
- YouTube Shorts: "Subscribe for part 2" (only if part 2 is guaranteed)
- Universal: "Follow for [specific ongoing series]" — tied to a repeatable format 

**Never**: "Like and follow for more content" — too generic, signals low value.
