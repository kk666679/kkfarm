## Core Philosophy

**Platforms are no longer the same product with different skins.** In 2026, each network behaves like a distinct media channel with its own economics, creative rules, and growth ceiling. A single vertical file that "just works everywhere" quietly gets throttled on most of them.

**The algorithmic divide has widened.** TikTok remains the dominant growth engine. Instagram is critical but harder — engagement is in structural decline. Facebook is stable but low-velocity. X has narrowed to a tactical channel. LinkedIn rewards depth over reach. Threads is becoming visual, fast, and conversation-driven.

**Format choice is goal-dependent.** The same platform rewards different formats for reach vs. engagement. Instagram carousels earn 12% more engagement than Reels — but Reels reach 36% more people. You cannot optimise for both with one post.

## TikTok

### The core truth

**Completion rate is king.** TikTok's For You ranker is a watch-time machine. The single heaviest input is how much of the video each viewer watches. A 20-second video watched fully beats a 90-second video watched halfway.

### Signal weights (relative reach impact)

| Signal | Weight | Note |
|--------|--------|------|
| Completion / full watch | **Highest** | Watch to the last frame |
| Rewatch / loop | Very high | A rewatch counts almost like a fresh view |
| Share (send to friend, repost) | High | Re-injects into a new follower graph |
| Save | High | Private "I will come back" signal |
| Comment | Medium-high | Real comment thread signals conversation |
| Follow from video | High (long-term) | One follow outweighs many likes |
| Like | **Low** | Cheap affirmation, light reach |
| Negative (swipe-away <2s, hide, report) | Heavy penalty | Fast swipe-away is the strongest negative signal |

Source:

### Length

| Content type | Optimal length |
|--------------|----------------|
| Trend participation | 7–15s |
| Product showcase | 15–30s |
| **Push / engagement sweet spot** | **21–34s** |
| Tips and tricks | 30–60s |
| Tutorials | 1–3 minutes |
| Deeper watch-time engagement | Up to 60s |

Above 90 seconds only if retention is genuinely there.

### Hashtags

**3–5 hashtags, mixed reach.** One broad tag (1M+ uses), two medium (100K–1M), one niche. Do not spam 15+ — it reads as spam and does not buy reach.

**Critical 2026 nuance:** The algorithm reads the video itself (visual content, on-screen text, audio) more heavily than the hashtag list. Hashtags are best used for **niche signaling rather than discovery**.

### First-frame rule

A visual hook in the first **600 milliseconds** and a verbal/caption hook by second **1.5**. TikTok's For You scoring weighs the first watch-loop heavily. No intro, logos, greetings, or slow zooms.

### Platform stats

- For You feed accounted for **70%+ of video views** by late 2025
- Median brand follower growth: **200%+ YoY**
- Generates **2× median interactions of Instagram** and **20× of Facebook**

## Instagram

### Reels

**Original, made-for-Instagram content wins.** The algorithm down-ranks anything that looks recycled from other platforms. Instagram stopped recommending accounts that primarily repost others' work — extending a policy previously limited to Reels to photos and carousels platform-wide.

#### Length

| Content type | Optimal length |
|--------------|----------------|
| Fastest loops | 7–20s |
| Retention-driven | 20–45s |
| Soft ceiling | 90s |
| Hard ceiling | 3 minutes (shown to non-followers) |

#### Signal weights

| Signal | Weight |
|--------|--------|
| Shares | **Highest** — sending to a friend signals real connection |
| Watch time / completion | Critical for Reels |
| Saves | High |
| Comments | Medium |
| Likes | Deprioritised |

Source:

#### Format performance

| Format | Median engagement | Reach multiplier |
|--------|------------------|------------------|
| Carousels | **6.90%** | 1× |
| Reels | 2.46–3.31% | **2.25×** more people than single images |
| Single images | 4.4% | Baseline |

Carousels drive **109% more engagement per person reached** than Reels — but Reels reach **2.25× more people**. Format choice is goal-dependent.

### Instagram Feed

- Carousels earn **114% more engagement** than single-image posts and **12% more than Reels**
- Carousels earn **22–23% more saves** than single photos
- Reach now depends on how people actually interact (watch time, shares, saves) — not follower count

### Hashtags

**3–5 highly relevant hashtags.** Instagram no longer rewards hashtag spam in 2026. Hashtags are capped at 5 — SEO (keyword in first line of caption, on-screen text, spoken once) is the new hashtag strategy.

### 2026 policy note

Instagram renamed its "AI Creator" label to **"AI-Generated Profile"** on 31 August 2026. Undisclosed AI-generated personas become **non-recommendable** across Explore, Reels, and feed suggestions. Proactive disclosure retains normal reach【from authenticity guide, platform policy section】.

## X (Twitter)

### Length

| Purpose | Optimal length |
|---------|----------------|
| Highest engagement rate | **100–200 characters** (1.09% ER) |
| Quick reads | 70–100 characters |
| Threads (teaching/list) | 5–9 tweets |
| Threads (bookmarkable) | 8–15 tweets |

Posts between 100 and 200 characters hit the highest engagement rate at **1.09%**. Posts under 100 characters get more impressions but lower engagement.

### Format performance

| Format | Median engagement rate |
|--------|----------------------|
| Text | **3.56%** |
| Images | 3.40% |
| Video | 2.96% |
| Links | 2.25% |

Text leads — but images have narrowed the gap to just 5%.

### Algorithm priorities

- **Reply-to-post ratio:** 3:1 for mid-tier accounts (1K–50K followers). Reply heavily
- **Posting cadence:** 3–5×/day for mid-tier; 5–10×/day for large (50K+)
- **Outbound links:** Keep them out of the main post body. Links count 23 chars and signal "leaving the platform"
- **Author replies:** Reply to early replies fast — author engagement back on a reply is a strong signal

### Hashtags

**1–2 hashtags max.** Overuse looks spammy and reduces engagement. Only use hashtags that actually fit the post.

### Strategic role

X is **best treated as a tactical channel rather than a growth driver**. Its strategic value has narrowed to conversations and real-time updates.

## LinkedIn

### The 360Brew shift

LinkedIn fundamentally changed in February 2026. The new ranking system, **360Brew**, reads each post for relevance, expertise, and dwell time instead of counting hashtags and reactions. It audits profile alignment before fully distributing content.

### What 360Brew rewards

| Signal | Weight | Note |
|--------|--------|------|
| **Dwell time** | Highest | 30+ seconds = maximum distribution |
| **Saves** | Very high | One save = **5× a like** |
| **Sends (DMs)** | Very high | Private shares signal lasting value |
| **Comment depth** | High | 15+ words = **2.5× algorithmic weight** |
| Likes | **Nearly worthless** | Cheap affirmation |

Source:

### Personal profiles vs. company pages

The data is stark. Personal posts (original employee-generated content) outperform company-curated posts by **9×**:

| Metric | Personal post | Curated post | Difference |
|--------|--------------|--------------|------------|
| Average total engagements | 62.74 | 6.94 | **9× more** |
| Average clicks | 23.99 | 2.65 | **9× more** |
| Average reactions | 36.55 | 4.16 | **8.8× more** |
| Average comments | 2.21 | 0.13 | **17× more** |

Source:

Company page organic posts appear in only **5.37%** of feed slots. Nearly a third of the feed is paid advertising. The most common reason a 2nd-degree connection's post appears in someone's feed is because a **1st-degree connection commented or reacted to it**.

### Format performance

| Format | Median engagement rate |
|--------|----------------------|
| **Carousels (PDF posts)** | **21.77%** — highest across all platforms |
| Native video | ~7.3% |
| Text | ~3.2% |

Carousels earn **196% more than video** and **585% more than text posts**.

### Length

Keep ~150 characters above the fold. The first 2 lines need to be magnetic — same rule as Threads. Dwell time of 30+ seconds maximises distribution.

### Hashtags

**1–3 targeted, professional hashtags.** Three is the sweet spot for max reach. Hashtags are used for categorisation, not discovery.

### 2026 enforcement

LinkedIn added a **"Seems like AI slop" reporting button** in July 2026. Flagged posts see around **40% fewer views**. 41% of longform LinkedIn posts were flagged as completely AI-generated【from authenticity guide, platform policy section】.

## Facebook

### Format performance

| Format | Median engagement rate |
|--------|----------------------|
| **Live video** | **4–8%** |
| Reels (short-form video) | 3–6% |
| Long-form video | 2–5% |
| Link posts | 1–2% |

Live video delivered roughly **4× the engagement of links** and **6× images**. Reels on Facebook see engagement rates **2–3× higher than static posts**.

### Algorithm priorities

| Signal | Weight |
|--------|--------|
| Shares | **Highest** |
| Comments (especially multi-reply threads) | High |
| Reactions beyond like | Medium-high |
| Video watch time | Medium |
| Likes | **Lowest** — nearly dismissed on large Pages |

Posts generating shares and extended comment threads spread **3–10× further** than posts generating only likes.

### Live video

Facebook Live gets an **algorithmic boost during broadcast**. 20–45 minute sessions drive real engagement from committed followers. **Comment velocity during Live** sends strong signals to the algorithm.

### Length

**40–80 characters** for engagement. Specific questions that invite real answers drive multi-paragraph replies. Generic questions ("What do you think?") underperform.

### Hashtags

**1–2 hashtags.** Less hashtag-driven than Instagram or TikTok.

### Posting cadence

3–5 posts per week, including at least 2 Reels, delivers better results than bursts followed by silence. The algorithm identifies audiences better when you post consistently at similar times.

## Threads

### The topic tag system

Threads **does not use hashtags**. It uses **topic tags** — a separate Meta concept that sorts your post into a topic feed.

- **1 topic tag per post.** This is by design — it prevents hashtag stuffing
- Topic tags are **interest-based markers**, not sorting tools. They work alongside enhanced search and recommendation systems to surface relevant content and communities you might not otherwise see
- Hashtags typed into the post body may provide minor searchability, but the **dedicated topic tag slot is what the algorithm uses for distribution**

### Algorithm priorities (2026)

| Signal | Weight | Note |
|--------|--------|------|
| **Engagement velocity** | Highest | Speed of first 100 reactions, not total count. Slow posts get capped early |
| **Reposts** | Strongest engagement signal | Reposts weigh more than likes |
| **Reply length** | High | Replies over 8 words count meaningfully; two-word "Same!" barely registers |
| **Image presence** | High | Posts with at least one image outperform text-only on For You reach |
| Likes | **Lowest of four reactions** | Deprioritised |

Source:

### Format performance

| Format | Median engagement rate |
|--------|----------------------|
| **Video** | **5.55%** |
| Images | ~4.6% |
| Text | 2.79% |

Video leads engagement at **5.55%** — nearly double text posts (2.79%) — on a platform primarily associated with conversation.

### Length

Threads truncates posts at **~4 lines**. The first 2 lines need to be magnetic — same rule as LinkedIn.

### Growth mechanics

- **Golden hour:** 0–4 hours after posting. If you do not get **30+ reactions in the first hour**, the algorithm caps you
- **Reply to your own thread within 90 seconds.** This doubles thread depth and is one of the cleanest ways to boost engagement velocity
- **Repost others to earn reposts.** Threads tracks reciprocity
- **Image posts dominate** For You reach. Even a screenshot, chart, or meme outperforms plain text
- **External links no longer kill reach.** The 2024 link penalty is gone

### High early engagement impact

High early engagement lifts reach by **+42% on Threads** — the highest of any platform (+30% LinkedIn, +21% Instagram).

## Anti-Patterns (2026 Updated)

### ❌ Never Do

- **Cross-post the same file everywhere** — each platform rewards different first frames, caption densities, sound behaviour, and hashtag logic
- **Hashtag stuffing** — 15+ hashtags reads as spam and does not buy reach on any platform
- **Ignore the first 1–3 seconds** — the hook decides the video on TikTok, Reels, and Shorts
- **Use hashtags on Threads** — use 1 topic tag; hashtags are decorative
- **Post links as primary content on Facebook** — links get 4× less engagement than Live video
- **Over-produce** — audiences distrust "too perfect" brand content【from authenticity guide】
- **Use engagement pods on LinkedIn** — 360Brew actively detects and renders them ineffective, cutting post visibility
- **Repost others' content on Instagram** — accounts that primarily repost lose recommendation eligibility
- **Post sporadically then in bursts** — behavioural anomaly flags trigger review

### ✅ Do Instead

- **Optimise for completion, saves, and shares** — not likes. On TikTok, a save is worth more than 10 likes
- **Lead with a visual on Threads** — even a screenshot outperforms plain text
- **Reply to your own thread within 90 seconds** on Threads — doubles thread depth
- **Use 100–200 characters on X** for highest engagement rate
- **Post carousels on LinkedIn** — 21.77% median engagement, the highest across all platforms
- **Go Live on Facebook** — 20–45 minute sessions get algorithmic boost during broadcast
- **Match format to goal** — Reels for reach, carousels for engagement
- **Post consistently at similar times** — the algorithm identifies audiences better with regularity
